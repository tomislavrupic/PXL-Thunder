RIDE THE STORM

Press Play to start the browser audio engine. No account, downloads or audio uploads.

  • Click a shape or trigger pad to play a voice. Drag its shape to change P1/P2.
  • Drag dials vertically; Alt-drag for fine adjustments. Keyboard arrows work too.
  • Click a step, then turn the existing knobs to save locks for that step. Click the selected step again to toggle its hit. Right-click opens ratchets, odds, probability and velocity.
  • A selected step can hold many locks, including Algo, Mode and filter type. Right-click a knob to clear only its lock. Click a voice trigger or name to edit the base sound across the sequence; existing step locks stay intact. The existing effects controls save shared FX locks for that step. Click the step label × or press Escape to edit the base sound again.
  • The dice icon generates only that voice's rhythm, preserving sounds, locks and timing. Shift-click the dice to undo its last generation.
  • SHIFT exposes rate, groove and direction. SHUFFLE edits swing; LAST edits track length. SHIFT + LAST edits master length.
  • SHIFT + SHUFFLE enables fills. Choose a rate in the first eight steps, then hold a trigger.
  • REC records taps and control changes. Space plays/stops; 1–4 triggers voices; Escape exits lock editing.
  • Patterns and kits have 64 slots per bank. Alt-click saved patterns to chain them.
